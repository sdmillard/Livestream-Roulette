# Wildlife feed shortlist

Reviewed November 22, 2026. The current wallpaper plays direct HTTPS video and HLS. A playable URL and permission to use it as a wallpaper are separate checks. The two Unified Streaming demos remain available for playback testing.

| Candidate | Published delivery and availability | Permission and next check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **AGH University kestrel nest** | The university-hosted directory identifies AGH's IT Solutions Centre as operator and lists a direct HTTPS H.264/MP4 endpoint. The directory reports a browser check on November 10. Current playback and live hours still need confirmation. | The directory explicitly grants no license. Ask the operator whether the direct endpoint may be used for unattended personal wallpaper playback and, separately, whether it may be listed as a default. [Directory](https://app.qoe.agh.edu.pl/public/kamery/en.html?lang=en) · [Operator contact](https://cri.agh.edu.pl/kontakt) |
| **Monterey Bay Aquarium Jelly Cam** | The Aquarium lists live hours of 07:00–19:00 Pacific and identifies off-hours footage as recorded. The official page points to YouTube and an authorized direct endpoint is unconfirmed. | The terms direct personal-use permission requests to `visitinfo@mbayaq.org`. Ask about an authorized non-YouTube endpoint and the preferred credit. [Jelly Cam](https://www.montereybayaquarium.org/cams-videos/live-cams/jelly-cam) · [Camera schedule](https://www.montereybayaquarium.org/cams-videos/live-cams) · [Terms](https://www.montereybayaquarium.org/about-us/terms-of-use) |
| **Barn Owl Live** | Barn Owl, BadgerWood, and MeadowView have publisher-supplied widgets. The browser test showed the widgets wrap YouTube. | The publisher explicitly allows website embeds. A separate direct source would need authorization for wallpaper playback. [Publisher embed guide](https://barnowl.live/resources/embed-our-live-barn-owl-nest-stream-in-your-website-its-easy-and-free/) · [YouTube developer policies](https://developers.google.com/youtube/terms/developer-policies-guide) |
| **NOAA Ocean Exploration** | Okeanos Explorer carries live ROV video during expeditions. NOAA's live page showed the ship in port at review time. Its outreach material mentions YouTube embeds and says some channels carry replays outside dives. | NOAA says its site media is generally public domain unless marked otherwise and requests the credit specified on the source. Confirm the specific expedition feed and a supported delivery option with NOAA. [Live status](https://oceanexplorer.noaa.gov/livestreams/) · [Usage and credit](https://oceanexplorer.noaa.gov/faqs/) · [Livestream outreach guide](https://oeab.noaa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Communication-Outreach-and-Education-Opportunities.pdf) |
| **Explore.org / Katmai Bearcam** | NPS identifies Explore.org as its partner. Brooks River bear cameras are seasonal, generally late June to early October. | Explore.org directs live-embed inquiries to `PR@explore.org`. Ask about permitted desktop playback and delivery before trying it as a wallpaper. [NPS camera information](https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/watch-the-katmai-bearcam.htm) · [Explore.org terms](https://explore.org/terms-of-service) |
| **Pennsylvania Game Commission / HDOnTap** | The commission links to HDOnTap for eagle, elk, and other wildlife cameras. HDOnTap supports publisher-configured embedded players with trusted hosts. | Ask the camera operator about a wallpaper deployment and an authorized delivery option. The vendor's embed instructions are for camera customers. [Camera list](https://www.pa.gov/agencies/pgc/education/wildlife-on-wifi/virtual-field-trips) · [HDOnTap embed security](https://help.hdontap.com/embed-security-trusted-hosts/) |

AGH is the first technical lead because a direct URL is published. Monterey is the first aquarium lead. Request permission for a private pilot separately from any inclusion in a distributed default catalog. If approved, record source credit and expected live hours, then verify playback, current footage, and behavior when the camera goes offline.
